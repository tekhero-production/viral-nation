# Content & Copywriting Guidelines

This document tracks the voice and tone required for Viral Nation.

## Tone of Voice
- **Calm, Elite, Effective.**
- Do not use slang or generic marketing buzzwords.
- Sound like a premium financial service or a leading management consultancy rather than a highly energetic start-up.

## Grammar & Structure
- **Short statements.** "Creators selected for impact."
- **Outcome-led.** Avoid "We do X", instead use "We build Y to achieve Z".
- Less is more. Allow the typography and spacing to do the communication. 

## Primary CTAs
- "Start a Campaign" (Global)
- "Launch a Live Campaign" (Live TikTok Page)
- "Explore Talents" (Home / Talent)
- "Become a Partner" (Partners)
